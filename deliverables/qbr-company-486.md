## Slide 2 — What's Going Well

**Major commercial expansion executed.** Customer signed for 65 seats at $12,935 MRR — a 16x increase from their previous engagement. Validates the value proposition at scale.

**Auto-renew enabled on Enterprise contract.** Procurement-level commitment to continued partnership.

**Support quality maintained.** Last 90 days: 1 ticket only, resolved in 14 hours. No escalations. Historical CSAT of 4/5 on rated tickets.

> *"The relationship has matured commercially. Now we need it to mature operationally."*

---

## Slide 3 — What Needs Attention

**1. Adoption gap.** 65 seats provisioned, ~6 active days of usage in 90. Engagement score 15/100. The expansion was sold but not rolled out. Risk: customer perceives no value at renewal review.

**2. Negative usage trend.** 92 events last 90 days vs 113 prior (-18%). Even before the new seats activate, existing users are tapering off.

**3. Pattern history.** Two prior churns. Last reason: "support → missing features." This customer churns when they feel underserved at renewal moments.

> *Auto-renew protects revenue for one cycle. It does not protect the relationship.*

## Slide 5 — The Ask

**1. Confirm the executive sponsor.** Who at Company_486 owns the success of this deployment? We need a named senior stakeholder for our quarterly exec sync.

**2. Commit to the rollout plan.** We have the seats, the budget, and the contract. We need internal champions inside your team to drive adoption across the 61 new seats over the next 60 days.

**3. Schedule a renewal pre-conversation for July 2025.** We want to walk into your renewal review with measurable ROI, not a price negotiation. That requires a value scorecard we co-build between now and Q2.

> *This is our third cycle with you. Our commitment is to make it our most successful one — and to be the partner you don't have to evaluate against alternatives next time.*
