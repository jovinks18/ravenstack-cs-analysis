# Customer Success Executive Summary
**RavenStack book review • Q1 2025 • Prepared by Jovin**

---

## The number

**$4.5M of active ARR is at risk in the next 12 months** — 38% of the $11.8M book.

This is the weighted exposure across 67 Red accounts ($1.9M) and a soft middle of 214 Yellow accounts (50% weighting = $2.6M).

---

## What's actually driving churn

We've already lost **$3.0M ARR to historical churn**. Where it went:

| Driver | ARR lost | % of churn | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget pressure | $600K | 31% | Sales + Finance |
| Missing features | $418K | 22% | Product |
| Pricing | $372K | 19% | Finance + Sales |
| Support quality | $343K | 18% | **CS + Support Ops** |
| Competitive losses | $109K | 6% | Product + Marketing |
| Reason not captured | $101K | 5% | CS process gap |

**Only 18% of historical churn (the "support" line) is something CS could have directly prevented.** The remaining 82% requires CS to influence Product, Sales, and Finance — not save individual accounts.

---

## The under-used asset: reactivation

**79% of churned accounts come back.** 277 of 352 ever-churned accounts are currently active, representing **$8.0M of recovered ARR**.

This is currently accidental. It should be a defined program with plays, owners, and conversion metrics. Sized correctly, win-back is likely the highest-ROI motion available to this CS team.

---

## What CS should do differently

**1. Reorient from saves to influence.** Build a quarterly *Churn Evidence Brief* delivered to Product and Finance leadership. Translate the 82% non-addressable churn drivers into roadmap and pricing input. This is CS earning a strategic seat at the table.

**2. Launch a structured win-back program.** Define triggers, owners, scripts, and exit criteria for reactivation. Target: convert the existing 79% accidental rate into a managed 85%+ with documented playbook.

**3. Renewal-stage CS, not lifecycle-stage CS.** Tenured accounts churn at higher rates than new ones in this book (371 vs 329 days avg tenure). Front-load CSM attention 90 days before renewal for accounts >$50K ARR.

**4. Tighten the soft middle.** 214 Yellow accounts ($5.2M ARR) are the strategic problem, not the Red tail. Define what "yellow" means operationally — a play, not a label — or the segmentation is noise.

---

## What we need from leadership

- **Endorsement** to deliver the Churn Evidence Brief quarterly to Product and Finance
- **Budget approval** for the win-back program (estimated 0.5 FTE + tooling)
- **Decision** on CSM coverage model: account-based for >$50K ARR, pooled for SMB

---

*Methodology note: V1 health score (weighted composite of usage, support, tenure, engagement, commercial signals) showed weak predictive lift against historical churn. This is itself the finding — behavioral telemetry alone is insufficient for this book. Recommendations are reason-code-anchored, not score-anchored.*
